Agropioo

==================================================
SUMMARY (50–200 words)
==================================================
Agropioo is an AI-powered, Pakistan-first farm decision-support platform that helps farmers decide what to do, when to do it, and why. We’ve built a working Next.js web/PWA with a multi-agent agriculture advisor (8 specialized agents), digital farm records, weather advisories, mandi price tracking and predictions, crop disease detection, crop recommendations, pest-risk alerts, and profit/loss tracking in 8 local languages.

For the Field Shift challenge, Agropioo includes an API-integrated Rotation Planner that connects to NASA Earth observation services for a farmer’s field (NASA POWER agroclimatology, SMAP soil moisture, GPM IMERG precipitation, and AppEEARS-served MODIS/VIIRS vegetation indices and ECOSTRESS evapotranspiration/heat stress). Combined with farm records, soil/crop constraints, and farmer priorities (water use, resilience, income, soil health), Agropioo compares at least three rotation strategies (including current practice), explains supporting evidence, highlights bare/vulnerable periods, and surfaces risks and data gaps for local expert review. This matters because timely, localized, explainable rotation guidance can reduce losses and strengthen climate resilience for smallholder farmers.

PROJECT DETAILS
==================================================
What it does and how it works (current)
Agropioo is a full-stack web/PWA platform (Next.js 16, React 19, Neon Postgres) that gives farmers a single place to manage their farm and get personalized guidance:
- Multi-agent AI advisor: 8 specialized agents (triage, crop, weather, prices, schemes, farm data, crop recommendation, handoff) using OpenAI Agents SDK with streaming responses, conversation memory, and RAG over 21 agricultural knowledge documents (pgvector embeddings).
- Digital farm records: structured, farm-specific logs (sowing, planting, irrigation, fertilizer, pesticide, disease, harvest, expenses/growth) that provide context to recommendations.
- Farm & weather intelligence: interactive farm mapping (Leaflet/React-Leaflet, geocoding), farm health/growth tracking, and farm-specific weather advisories/alerts (OpenWeather-based).
- Market & planning tools: mandi price tracking from 5 Pakistan government sources with history/predictions and SELL/HOLD signals, a crop recommendation engine (40+ crops, 16 district soil profiles, rotation rules), pest-outbreak risk alerts, disease detection (photo upload, TensorFlow.js + HuggingFace, Cloudinary storage), and profit/loss/break-even tracking.
- Accessibility & resilience: 8 languages (en, ur, pa, ps, sd, skr, bal, hno) with RTL support, database-managed translations, and offline-first PWA support (Serwist + IndexedDB write queue).

How it addresses the Field Shift challenge (API-based integration)
Agropioo includes a “Rotation Planner” decision-support module that compares at least three rotation strategies (including the farmer’s current rotation) for a selected field and season window:
- API-based data integration (no manual bulk dataset downloads): server-side Next.js Route Handlers connect to NASA Earth observation services via APIs for location/time-based queries:
  - NASA POWER API for agroclimatology (temperature, precipitation, humidity, solar/radiation, frost/growing-season indicators).
  - SMAP (via NASA Earthdata services) for surface soil moisture and drought-stress signals.
  - GPM IMERG (via NASA Earthdata/GES DISC services) for precipitation estimates, rainfall reliability/extremes, and field moisture inputs.
  - NASA AppEEARS API for field-level MODIS/VIIRS vegetation indices (NDVI/vigor, stress, bare-ground periods) and ECOSTRESS land-surface temperature/evapotranspiration (heat stress, crop-water demand).
- Data fusion & analysis: harmonize indicators to a common spatial/temporal view as needed and combine them with on-farm records/history, soil properties, crop characteristics, irrigation/water availability, and farmer priorities (conserve water, improve soil structure, supplemental income, erosion reduction, market suitability).
- Explainable recommendations: for each strategy show why it was suggested, supporting evidence (indicator summaries/time trends), potential benefits (water efficiency, infiltration/erosion reduction, soil health, pest-cycle interruption, income options), uncertainties/data gaps, and risks requiring local farmer/agronomist review. Highlight vulnerable and bare-field periods.
- Farmer-centered design: clear local-language explanations of terms (evapotranspiration, NDVI, soil-moisture anomaly), priority inputs, and explicit reminders that the tool supports—not replaces—farmer judgment and local expertise.

Benefits and intended impact
- Helps farmers explore climate-resilient, water-smart rotation options grounded in observable field conditions and farm history.
- Makes satellite/environmental insights actionable and explainable in local context/languages, reducing avoidable losses and improving long-term soil and livelihood resilience.
- Builds on a working, localized platform so guidance is tied to actual farm data and priorities, adaptable to other regions by swapping local crops, soils, practices, and data sources.

Tools, languages, hardware, and software used
- App: TypeScript, Next.js 16 (App Router, Route Handlers), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Leaflet/React-Leaflet.
- Backend/data: Neon Lakebase Postgres (+ pgvector), Zod, bcryptjs/jose auth, nodemailer/SMTP, Cloudinary.
- AI/ML: OpenAI Agents SDK (@openai/agents) with gpt-4o-mini, RAG embeddings via Ollama nomic-embed-text (768-dim), TensorFlow.js + HuggingFace for disease detection.
- APIs/services (current): OpenWeather, 5 Pakistan government mandi/price sources (AMIS, SAMIS, FMIS KP, BMIS, PBS-SPI), Photon/geocoding; Serwist PWA/offline.
- Field Shift module: NASA POWER API, SMAP/GPM IMERG access via NASA Earthdata services, and NASA AppEEARS API (MODIS/VIIRS, ECOSTRESS) integrated via server-side Route Handlers.

Creativity and factors considered
- Connects a working farmer-facing system (records + advisor + market/weather tools) with EO-driven, explainable rotation analysis tied to actual farm history and priorities.
- Compares multiple strategies (including current practice), emphasizes uncertainty and human-in-the-loop review, and considers water constraints, erosion/terrain, heat/rainfall stress, bare-field windows, and market/practical feasibility alongside soil-health goals.
